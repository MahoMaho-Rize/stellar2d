## Findings (paper-ready)

### 1. Ṁ_e converges across scheme families at N ≥ 256²
All 13 saturated runs (vl2 256/512 × 2 schemes + ale2 256/512 × 7 schemes) cluster in the range **1.0 × 10⁻⁵ to 2.0 × 10⁻⁵ per Lx**, a spread of factor 2. 3D reference 2.37×10⁻⁵ sits within factor 2 of every single point.

vl2 Ṁ_e ratio 256→512: 1.46e-5 → 1.52e-5 (VL, 4%), 1.54e-5 → 1.51e-5 (MM, -2%) — **converged**.
ale2 Ṁ_e spread at 512: 1.19e-5 to 1.57e-5 — factor 1.3×.

### 2. v_rms is scheme-family-determined, brackets 3D
- Eulerian Godunov (vl2): v_rms 0.14–0.17, **above** 3D ref by factor 2×, converges with resolution (VL: 0.172 → 0.165 → 0.160)
- Lagrangian ALE (ale2): v_rms 0.02–0.04, **below** 3D ref by factor 3×, converges with resolution
- **3D ref 0.088 sits between the two families** — bracketing, not agreement

vl2 PLM-MM shows sub-convergence at 128² (v_rms=0.02, convection fails) that recovers at 256²+ (0.11 → 0.14). This is a minimum-resolution threshold for the minmod limiter.

### 3. δ_int scales with grid; ale2 is always sharper than vl2
- vl2 δ_int: 2.40 (128) → 0.43 (256) → 0.23 (512)  [~Δx²⁻¹ scaling]
- ale2 δ_int: already at 0.05–0.09 by 256², barely shrinks to 0.05 at 512² — saturated at grid scale
- vl2/ale2 ratio: always ~4–5× (Godunov interface is 4–5× wider than ALE interface)

### 4. L_iso/Lx (wrinkling factor) settles near 1.1–1.3 at saturation
Both families produce similar wrinkling at 256²+, despite very different interface thickness. Interpretation: vortex-mediated folding is a robust 2D observable.

### 5. Classical entrainment balance Ṁ_e ≈ ρ·D_eff·A/δ is consistent
Computing D_eff = Ṁ_e · δ / (ρ·L_iso·Lx) for each run:
- vl2 512: D_eff ≈ 2–3 × 10⁻⁶ (large δ compensated by large D)
- ale2 512: D_eff ≈ 0.5–0.6 × 10⁻⁶ (small δ compensated by small D)
- **Factor 4–5× difference in D_eff, factor 5× in δ — product Ṁ_e differs by factor ~1 as observed.**

### 6. 128² is under-resolved in both families
- ale2 128: interface collapses by t ≈ 500 (X becomes uniform)
- vl2 128 MM: convection fails entirely; y_ub drifts to top
- ale2 128 early-time (t<500) still gives v_rms, Ṁ_e in the "right ballpark" before interface dies — useful as lower bound only

## Paper-ready narrative (one-sentence)

> Mass entrainment rate in the 2D Andrassy+ 2022 O-shell benchmark converges to within a factor of 2 across Eulerian Godunov (Athena-style PLM+HLLC+vl2) and Lagrangian ALE (Caramana-style swept-remap) schemes at N ≥ 256², agreeing with the 3D reference Ṁ_e within the same tolerance, even though the mean convective velocity differs by a factor of 5 between scheme families and brackets the 3D value from opposite sides.

## Caveats / open issues (to investigate before submission)

1. **vl2 Ṁ_e looks "too good"** — 3 data points (256-VL, 256-MM, 512-VL, 512-MM) are all at 1.5e-5 with almost no scatter (<5%). This is suspicious given Andrassy 2022 reported 10% cross-code spread in 3D and we have 2 wildly different v_rms values. **Possible cause**: our M_e measurement in the low-entrainment regime is saturating on the geometry (conv layer is filled with ε·X regardless of true entrainment process). Need to check.

2. **ale2 128 Ṁ_e = 5e-5** is 3× above the 3D ref but ALE 256/512 converge to 1–2e-5. Early-time 128² is dominated by an initial transient where X is being mixed across the numerically broadened interface, not by true entrainment. Should exclude 128² from paper Ṁ_e comparison or flag clearly.

3. **δ vs Δx law not explicitly verified**: we see δ drop by ~2× from 256→512 for vl2 VL (expected for linear scaling), but ale2 shows δ saturated. Need to check if ale2 δ is truly at grid scale (would be δ=Δy=Ly/ny=2/512≈0.004, but we measure δ≈0.05 — 12× grid scale — so there's still physics in ale2 δ).

4. **Tracer IC bug in athena_vl2** (discovered late): `eta1(y_paper)` was called with `yc_slab` so X was zeroed across most of the domain, dropping species_mass from 0.245 to 9.8×10⁻⁴. Fixed in commit (pending) and all 6 vl2 runs re-run as `athena_vl2_scan_v2`. Old `athena_vl2_scan/` v1 runs are stale — delete before committing.

5. **Possible code bugs to investigate** (per user request):
   - Check ale2 interface collapse at 128: is it numerical or physical (CFL, AV, remap order, limiter clamp)?
   - Check vl2 PLM-MM at 128²: convection completely fails — is the minmod limiter path in our kernels overly dissipative vs Athena's own implementation?
   - Re-verify bit-identical IC between ale2 and vl2 at first frame (should be identical to machine precision now that species_mass matches 0.2449).
