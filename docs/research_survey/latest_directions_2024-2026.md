## 1. Suwa — NEW 2024-2026 directions

**A. Failed-SN / BH-formation neutrino phenomenology (NEW, 2025).**
- `2504.19510` [1st] "Neutrino Constraints on Black Hole Formation in M31"
  — applies the BLEND template to a *failed* collapse, predicting the
  short intense ν-burst that truncates at BH formation.
- Distinction vs 2020-2023: Papers I-V of the Super-K series (2008.07070,
  2108.03009, 2204.08363, 2304.05437, 2404.18248) inferred PNS M/R/distance
  from *successful* CCSN neutrinos. 2025 extends the same pipeline into
  the failed-SN regime motivated by the observed M31-2014-DS1 candidate.

**B. Fallback → compact-remnant mass function (NEW, 2025).**
- `2512.11404` [mid, last=Suzuki] — 1D Athena++ sweep of explosion-energy
  vs H-envelope binding energy, finds a universal 2-3× E_bind threshold
  above which reverse shock escapes; yields an analytic M_remnant(E_exp)
  prescription for population synthesis.
- Distinction: the pre-2024 56Ni-problem series (2301.03610, 2010.05615)
  asked what the innermost *ejectable* mass is; 2512.11404 inverts the
  question and asks what stays *bound*, for BH-mass-distribution use.

**C. SN ↔ planet-formation coupling (NEW, 2025).**
- `2512.09660` [mid] "Cosmic-Ray Bath in a Past Supernova…" (Science
  Advances) — nearby-SN cosmic-ray flux as SLR / ionisation source for
  terrestrial-planet-forming disks.
- Distinction vs 2312.01948 (²⁶Al injection, 2023): that earlier paper
  required direct ejecta capture; 2025 relaxes the geometry via cosmic-ray
  transport, broadening the nearby-SN window considerably.

**D. Data-analysis hardening for SK operations (NEW, 2025).**
- `2505.19721` [mid] Super-K paper VI — injects realistic reactor +
  spallation backgrounds into BLEND. Operational-readiness step beyond
  the idealised papers III/V.

## 2. Suzuki — NEW 2024-2026 directions

**A. Non-ideal (ambipolar/Ohmic) MHD in cool-star / chromospheric winds
(NEW, 2024-2025).**
- `2501.00294` [1st] Red-giant winds with ambipolar diffusion — α Boo Ṁ
  matches observation, 1/15× ideal-MHD.
- `2403.18409` [2nd] Chromospheric ambipolar damping suppresses solar
  wind mass flux.
- Distinction vs 2020-2023 Alfvén-wave work (2203.15280, 2209.10156):
  ideal MHD baseline is now replaced by non-ideal partially-ionised
  transport as the dominant dissipation knob.

**B. Magnetically-driven-wind disk theory applied to TDEs (NEW, 2024-2025).**
- `2411.00298` [last] steady-state MDW thin disk.
- `2502.12549` [last] self-similar MDW disk applied to TDE light curves.
- Distinction vs 2312.15415 (2023, already had 1D time-dep TDE disks):
  the 2024-2025 pair formalises the *analytic* self-similar family that
  reshapes the late-time t^{-5/3} law; new theoretical scaffolding rather
  than a single numerical application.

**C. Protostellar spin-down via magnetospheric ejection (NEW, 2024).**
- `2412.14981` [mid] Takasao+ spin-down without invoking disk locking
  alone; combines magnetospheric ejections with Yorke-Bodenheimer-type
  PMS evolution.
- Distinction vs 2211.13522 (main-sequence braking weakening): 2024 moves
  the braking question back to the protostellar phase where the disk is
  still present.
