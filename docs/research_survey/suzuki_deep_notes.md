# Suzuki group — deep method notes (3 papers)

Target: map their methods onto stellar2d (1D Lagrangian implicit hydro, 2D ALE periodic, 2D pseudo-spectral NS, 2D anelastic g-mode EVP; **no MHD**, species tracers + variable g + custom heating available).

## Paper 1 — Suzuki, Ohnaka, Yasuda 2025 (arXiv:2501.00294)
"Red Giant Winds Driven by Alfvén Waves with Magnetic Diffusion"

- **Numerical method**: 1D spherically-symmetric, time-dependent, **nonideal MHD** along a single super-radially open magnetic flux tube. Explicit **second-order Godunov–MoCCT** (Sano et al. 1999) — Suzuki/Inutsuka in-house 1D stellar-wind code, lineage of Suzuki & Inutsuka 2005/2006, Matsuoka+2024 (M24). Ohmic + ambipolar diffusion added as explicit transverse-field diffusion term.
- **Grid and BC**: radial domain `R_*` (photosphere at `T = T_eff`) → `31 R_*`. Cell size Δr arranged so ≥4 points per local Alfvén wavelength `v_A/ω_max`. Fiducial α Boo run: Δr ≈ 5e-4 R_* at surface, N ≈ 1800 cells (2500–10000 across the run grid). Inner BC: photospheric ρ_0 scaled from ATLAS via `ρ_0 ∝ g^0.6 T_eff^{-2}`; transverse velocity/B perturbations injected via outgoing Elsässer `z⁺`. Outer BC: supersonic outflow (free).
- **Time integration**: explicit 2nd-order MoCCT with CFL set by fast-magnetosonic + resolved Alfvén wavelength; run duration `t_sim = 30 R_*/c_{s,0}` ≈ 1135 d (α Boo), 2144 d (α Tau). Time average on `[0.1 t_sim, t_sim]`.
- **Microphysics**: Braginskii thermal conduction (κ₀=1e-6 cgs), Spitzer-flux-limited by density. Radiative cooling: Sutherland–Dopita ionized (T > 1.2e4 K) + empirical chromospheric cooling (Anderson & Athay 1989, metallicity-scaled) for T ≤ 1.2e4 K, artificially cut below `T_cut = 0.7 T_eff`. Ionization: LTE Saha for H, He, plus low-FIP metals (C, O, Na, Mg, Al, Si, S, K, Ca, Cr, Fe), interstellar radiation term included. Ohmic η_O (Blaes & Balbus 1994) and ambipolar η_AD (Shu 1992 / Draine 1983) computed from `x_e`.
- **Drivers/source**: photospheric δv_⊥ (1.25 km/s solar value, scaled as `δv_0 ∝ (T_eff⁴/ρ_0)^{1/3}`) injected as **broadband Alfvén-wave spectrum** `P(ω) ∝ ω⁻¹` between `ω_min ≈ c_s/R_*` and `ω_max = 100 ω_min` (period ≈ 150–340 min). Alfvén-wave turbulent dissipation (Shoda+2018a) via Elsässer cascade, `D ∝ c_d |z⁺||z⁻|/λ_⊥`, `c_d=0.1`.
- **IC**: hydrostatic atmosphere of a K-giant (Arcturus α Boo: M=1.0 M☉, R=25.4 R☉, T_eff=4300K, log g=1.6, Z=0.3 Z☉) / Aldebaran (α Tau: 1.3 M☉, 45.2 R☉, 3900 K).
- **Main diagnostic**: time-averaged mass-loss rate `⟨Ṁ⟩ = 4πρ v_r r² f` vs. magnetic field strength, metallicity Z, and with/without ambipolar diffusion. Key result: nonideal MHD `⟨Ṁ⟩ = 3.3e-11 M☉/yr` for α Boo (matches observed 2.5–4e-11), ~1/15 of ideal-MHD value. Also X-ray luminosity, radial profiles of v_r/ρ/T/x_e (Figs. 2, 5, 7).
- **Key equations**: (10)–(14) spherical MHD with super-radial factor f(r); (7) Kopp–Holzer filling factor; (18)–(20) Elsässer turbulent dissipation; (26)–(27) η_O, η_AD; (29) Ṁ; (30) Reimers comparison; (36) X-ray luminosity integral.
- **What pure-hydro stellar2d can reproduce**: mostly not — the driver is Alfvén-wave Poynting flux. However, the radial 1D geometry with super-radial expansion maps onto radial1d. With a **prescribed heating Q(r,t)** emulating wave dissipation and an **imposed wind-acceleration body force** `F_mag(r) = -∂(B_⊥²/8π)/∂r`, radial1d could reproduce the temperature / density / v_r profile qualitatively, but **not the Ṁ dependence on B_r,0, Z, ambipolar diffusion** — those are intrinsically MHD.

## Paper 2 — Shimizu, Shoda, Suzuki 2022 (arXiv:2203.15280)
"Role of Longitudinal Waves in Alfvén-wave-driven Solar Wind"

- **Numerical method**: 1D spherically-symmetric MHD in a super-radially expanding open flux tube, r = R☉ to ≈100 R☉. Same Suzuki/Inutsuka 2nd-order Godunov–MoCCT code family (cites Shoda & Takasao 2021 for derivation). Ideal MHD + Alfvén-wave turbulence phenomenological dissipation + Spitzer–Härm thermal conduction + radiative cooling.
- **Grid and BC**: r ∈ [R☉, r_out ≈ 40–100 R☉]. Log-like telescoping grid: Δr = max[20 km, min(2000 km, (2ε_ge/(2+ε_ge))(r−r_ge)+20 km)] with ε_ge=0.01, r_ge=1.04 R☉ → 20 km at surface, 2000 km outer (Fig. 2). Inner BC at photosphere: T=T☉=5770 K, ρ̄☉=1.88e-7 g/cm³; `z⁻` free (absorbed, ∂z⁻/∂r=0). Outer BC: free outflow. Transverse and longitudinal perturbations injected as time-dependent BC on outgoing z⁺ and δv_∥.
- **Time integration**: explicit; sim time 4500–6000 min, averaged over final 1500 min.
- **Microphysics**: Spitzer–Härm conductivity `κ₀ T^{5/2}`, flux-limited below ρ_cnd=1e-20 g/cm³. Radiative cooling: interpolation of optically thick (Gudiksen–Nordlund Newton relaxation to T_ref=T☉ with τ_thick) and optically thin (Goodman–Judge chromospheric + CHIANTI coronal). **EOS**: partially ionized hydrogen with **Saha-Boltzmann ionization**, latent heat `n_H χ I_H` retained in internal energy (`e_int = p/(γ−1) + n_H χ I_H`).
- **Drivers/source**: broadband **both transverse and longitudinal** photospheric perturbations. Transverse: `z⁺_{θ,φ}(t) ∝ Σ_N sin(2π f_N t + φ_N)/√f_N`, 1e-3–1e-2 Hz (100 s to 1000 s periods), ⟨δv_⊥⟩ = 0.6 km/s. Longitudinal (the new ingredient, emulating p-modes): density and v_r injection, 3.3e-3–1e-2 Hz, amplitude ⟨δv_∥⟩ scanned 0–3 km/s. Alfvén-wave turbulence dissipation via Elsässer cascade (c_d=0.1, `λ_⊥,☉=150 km`).
- **IC**: hydrostatic stratification with T=T☉ everywhere initially, matched to power-law outer atmosphere `ρ = max[ρ☉ exp(−(r−R☉)/H), ρ_w,0 (r/R☉−1)^{-2.5}]`.
- **Main diagnostic**: `Ṁ_w = 4π r² f^op ρ v_r` vs. photospheric longitudinal amplitude ⟨δv_∥,☉⟩. **Fig. 5**: `ΔṀ_w ≈ 1.41 ⟨δv_∥,☉⟩^1.05 × 10⁻¹⁴ M☉/yr`, saturating above ≈2.7 km/s (up to 4× baseline). Mechanism: chromospheric **longitudinal→transverse mode conversion** boosts coronal Alfvén-wave Poynting flux, which heats the chromosphere-evaporation layer → denser corona → larger Ṁ. Also surface-integrated energy fluxes L_K, L_E, L_A, L_C, L_G, L_R at coronal base.
- **Key equations**: (3)–(8) spherical MHD with f^op(r) in expanding flux tube; (19)–(22) Saha EOS with latent heat; (23)–(24) Elsässer-Shoda turbulent dissipation terms; (29)–(33) two-step flux-tube expansion f_1^exp · f_2^exp (Shoda+2020); (44) Ṁ_w; (46) power-law fit; (47)–(59) energy-flux bookkeeping.
- **What pure-hydro stellar2d can reproduce**: 1D longitudinal-wave part is **directly mappable onto radial1d**. Inject sinusoidal v_r at inner boundary, include Spitzer-type conductivity and radiative cooling (already have MLT + rad coupling) and partially-ionized EOS (radial1d has Helm, could be extended with ionization latent heat). Would reproduce chromospheric spiculation/shock structure and wind from *acoustic shock heating alone* (Appendix A of the paper — BsV06 no-Alfvén case: T only reaches few×10⁵ K, no steady outflow). **Cannot** reproduce Alfvén/longitudinal mode-conversion coupling or the main Ṁ-enhancement physics. Good as a hydrostatic-atmosphere + acoustic-shock-heating benchmark on radial1d.

## Paper 3 — Taki, Kuwabara, Kobayashi, Suzuki 2020 (arXiv:2004.08839)
"New growth mechanism of dust grains in protoplanetary disks with magnetically driven disk winds"

- **Numerical method**: **1D radial, vertically-integrated axisymmetric two-fluid (gas + single-size dust) disk evolution** with a coagulation equation for the peak mass. Time-implicit gas update, explicit first-order upwind advection of dust. **No explicit MHD** — this is a *1D surface-density evolution code* that treats the disk wind as a parameterized sink term. Not an Athena-type grid code; lineage from Suzuki+2010/2016, Takahashi & Muto 2018.
- **Grid and BC**: r ∈ [0.01 au, 300 au], 255 log-spaced cells (Δr ∝ log r). Inner/outer BC: only outflow allowed (non-gradient when v_g,r<0 at r_in or >0 at r_out, else v_g,r=0). Dust Σ_d=0 inside 1.5 au (snowline proxy) and outside 200 au.
- **Time integration**: gas `∂Σ_g/∂t` solved with long implicit time step; dust Σ_d and peak mass m_p advanced explicitly with first-order upwind on a shorter Δt set by coagulation timescale.
- **Microphysics**: thin-disk Shakura–Sunyaev with ᾱ_rφ (turbulent viscosity, 8e-3 MRI-active or 8e-5 MRI-inactive), ᾱ_φz (MDW wind torque), and C_W (MDW mass-loss coefficient). Temperature fixed (passive): `T = 280 (r/au)^{-1/2} K` (Hayashi MMSN). Dust drag: Epstein / Stokes regime switching, Stokes number `St`, radial drift velocity (Adachi+1976, Nakagawa+1986). Coagulation: perfect sticking, single-size approximation (Sato+2016), relative velocities from Brownian + radial/azimuthal/vertical drift + turbulence (Ormel–Cuzzi 2007 two-class formula).
- **Drivers/source**: MDW wind mass loss `(ρ_g v_g,z)_w = C_W (ρ_g c_s)_mid` with C_W ≈ 1e-5 (from Suzuki+2010 local MHD), and wind torque `ᾱ_φz = ᾱ_φz,0 (Σ_g/Σ_g,0)^{-l}` with two choices (l=0 constant, l=0.66 Σ-dependent).
- **IC**: MMSN: Σ_g(r,0) = 1700 (r/au)^{-3/2} g/cm², Σ_d,0 = Σ_g,0/100, initial dust radius a₀ = 1e-4 cm. Total gas mass ≈0.04 M☉, dust ≈103 M⊕.
- **Main diagnostic**: time evolution (0, 10⁴, 10⁵, 10⁶ yr) of `Σ_g(r), Σ_d(r), P_mid(r), a(r), St(r)`. **Key result** (Figs. 3, 5): for MRI-inactive + MDW-on cases, an **inside-out pressure-bump-like or density-plateau forms at r≈1.5 au, dust grains grow to St>1 and pile up into a ring containing planetesimal-sized (km) bodies**. Condition: `Σ_d/Σ_g ≳ η` (dimensionless pressure gradient). Fig. 5 for WTb+WM+STq shows ring formation without an actual pressure bump.
- **Key equations**: (1)–(6) gas Σ_g viscous-wind evolution with Shakura–Sunyaev + MDW torque; (7)–(10) dust Σ_d and radial drift; (14) mass growth dm_p/dt; (16) Δv_pp with Brownian / turbulent / differential drift; (24)–(25) growth timescale `t_grow ∝ (Σ_g/Σ_d)/Ω_K`; `St = π ρ_m a / (2 Σ_g) · max(1, 4a/(9λ_mfp))`.
- **What pure-hydro stellar2d can reproduce**: **this is a radically different regime** (surface-density evolution, not 2D hydrodynamic simulation). None of the stellar2d solvers are a natural fit. You could, in principle, build a 1D-radial ODE driver on top of radial1d's Lagrangian infrastructure treating gas as an isothermal thin disk with `α`-viscosity + mass-sink `C_W` and **advect a passive-tracer species as dust**, using the existing species tracer. That would reproduce Fig. 1's `Σ_g(r,t)` inside-out dispersal qualitatively, but the **dust-growth feedback (Stokes-number evolution, radial drift reversal, ring formation)** requires dust momentum and a coagulation equation that stellar2d has no primitives for. Not worth pursuing in stellar2d; this paper's model is an O(10²)-cell MOL disk code, better ported as a standalone Python/C++ tool.
