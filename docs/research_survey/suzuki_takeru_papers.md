## 2025

- **arXiv:2512.11404** — "Impacts of hydrogen envelope on supernova fallback and the resulting compact remnant masses"
  - Authors: Shinoda, K., Suwa, Y., Hirai, R., Sawada, R., Tomida, K., Iwasaki, K., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: MNRAS (submitted), 2025
  - Role: **LAST**
  - Summary: 1D stellar-explosion simulations of 18–28 M☉ metal-poor progenitors show a threshold where explosion energy of 2–3× the envelope binding energy sends a reverse shock back to the center, producing remnants ≥ 2 M☉ heavier than the stripped-progenitor case.

- **arXiv:2502.12549** — "Self-Similar Solutions for Geometrically Thin Accretion Disks with Magnetically Driven Winds: Application to Tidal Disruption Events"
  - Authors: Tamilan, M., Hayasaki, K., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: PTEP, 2025
  - Role: **LAST**
  - Summary: Constructs analytical self-similar disk+wind solutions and applies them to TDE light-curve shapes, showing that magnetically driven mass loss reshapes the late-time decay relative to the classic t^{-5/3} law.

- **arXiv:2501.00294** — "Red Giant Winds Driven by Alfvén Waves with Magnetic Diffusion"
  - Authors: Suzuki, T. K., Ohnaka, K., Yasuda, Y.
  - Journal: PASJ, 2025
  - Role: **FIRST**
  - Summary: 1D Alfvén-wave-driven wind simulations for red-giant atmospheres, now including ambipolar-diffusion dissipation, reproduce observed cool-wind mass-loss rates and explain the corona/cool-wind dividing line through wave leakage.

## 2024

- **arXiv:2412.14981** — "Spin-down of solar-mass protostars in magnetospheric accretion paradigm"
  - Authors: Takasao, S., Kunitomo, M., Suzuki, T. K., Iwasaki, K., Tomida, K.
  - Journal: ApJ (accepted), 2024
  - Role: **MID**
  - Summary: Combines magnetospheric-ejection angular-momentum loss with protostellar-evolution models to show that class-II T Tauri stars can be spun down to observed slow rotators without invoking disk locking alone.

- **arXiv:2411.00298** — "Steady-State Solutions for a Geometrically Thin Accretion Disk with Magnetically-Driven Winds"
  - Authors: Tamilan, M., Hayasaki, K., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: PTEP, 2024/2025
  - Role: **LAST**
  - Summary: Sets up vertically integrated 1D α-disk equations augmented by a wind-mass-loss term derived from local shearing-box simulations; yields truncated-disk profiles that connect standard Shakura–Sunyaev to magnetic-braking regimes.

- **arXiv:2407.05093** — "Spiral Magnetic Field and Their Role on Accretion Dynamics in the Circumnuclear Disk of Sagittarius A*"
  - Authors: Sato, K., Shinnaga, H., Furuya, R. S., Suzuki, T. K., Ott, J., ...
  - Journal: 2024 (submitted)
  - Role: **MID**
  - Summary: ALMA dust-polarization observations of the Sgr A* circumnuclear disk reveal a spiral magnetic-field geometry that the authors argue drives magnetic braking and feeds accretion toward the central SMBH.

- **arXiv:2403.18409** — "Effect of Magnetic diffusion in the Chromosphere on the Solar Wind"
  - Authors: Matsuoka, M., Suzuki, T. K., Tokuno, T., Kakiuchi, K.
  - Journal: ApJ (submitted), 2024
  - Role: **MID** (2nd author, senior guidance)
  - Summary: 1D coronal-wind simulations with chromospheric ambipolar / Ohmic diffusion as a tunable parameter show that high diffusion damps high-frequency Alfvén waves before they reach the corona, suppressing wind mass flux.

- **arXiv:2403.07375** — "A Novel Method to Constrain Tidal Quality Factor from A Non-synchronized Exoplanetary System"
  - Authors: Tokuno, T., Fukui, A., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: ApJ (accepted), 2024
  - Role: **LAST**
  - Summary: Derives a statistical constraint on stellar Q_* by comparing TRAPPIST-1-like systems' observed spin–orbit states against tidal-evolution models, placing a lower bound Q_* > 10^6.

- **arXiv:2401.03733** — "Dynamics Near the Inner Dead-Zone Edges in a Protoplanetary Disk"
  - Authors: Iwasaki, K., Tomida, K., Takasao, S., Okuzumi, S., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: PASJ, 2024
  - Role: **LAST** (senior co-author)
  - Summary: 3D non-ideal MHD stratified-shearing-box simulations of the MRI-active / dead-zone transition reveal a persistent large-scale toroidal field that launches vertical disk winds and triggers episodic inner-edge accretion bursts.

## 2023

- **arXiv:2312.15415** — "Evolution of Tidal Disruption Event Disks with Magnetically Driven Winds"
  - Authors: Tamilan, M., Hayasaki, K., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: ApJ, 2024
  - Role: **LAST**
  - Summary: 1D time-dependent TDE-disk simulations with a physically motivated wind-loss term predict TDE light curves that decay faster than canonical fallback and better match the observed X-ray/UV transients at intermediate times.

- **arXiv:2306.15761** — "MHD Simulation in Galactic Center Region with Radiative Cooling and Heating"
  - Authors: Kakiuchi, K., Suzuki, T. K., Inutsuka, S., Inoue, T., Shimoda, J.
  - Journal: ApJ (accepted), 2024
  - Role: **LAST** (senior co-author)
  - Summary: Global 3D MHD simulation of the inner Galaxy with explicit radiative cooling shows that magnetic-loop buoyancy drives filamentary cold gas back down, regulating the central-molecular-zone mass budget via self-sustained turbulence.

- **arXiv:2305.12112** — "MHD in a cylindrical shearing box II: Intermittent Bursts and Substructures in MRI Turbulence"
  - Authors: Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: ApJ, 2023
  - Role: **FIRST** (sole author)
  - Summary: Long-duration cylindrical-shearing-box MHD simulations find that MRI-driven turbulence in a geometrically thin disk develops periodic accretion bursts and ring/gap substructure without any external trigger, purely from toroidal-field reversal cycles.

- **arXiv:2303.17469** — "Coronal Loops with Different Metallicities and Generalized RTV Scaling Laws"
  - Authors: Washinoue, H., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: ApJ, 2023
  - Role: **LAST**
  - Summary: 1D loop hydrodynamics with metallicity-dependent radiative loss extends the Rosner-Tucker-Vaiana scaling to sub-solar metallicity, predicting longer / hotter coronal loops in Population II stars.

## 2022

- **arXiv:2212.10796** — "Collisional Growth and Fragmentation of Dust Aggregates. II. Mass Distribution of Icy Fragments"
  - Authors: Hasegawa, Y., Suzuki, T. K., Tanaka, H., Kobayashi, H., Wada, K.
  - Journal: ApJ (accepted), 2023
  - Role: **MID** (2nd author)
  - Summary: N-body monomer-level dust-aggregate collision simulations produce a power-law mass distribution of icy fragments that feeds back into protoplanetary-disk coagulation modeling.

- **arXiv:2211.13522** — "Transition of latitudinal differential rotation as a possible cause of weakened magnetic braking of solar-type stars"
  - Authors: Tokuno, T., Suzuki, T. K., Shoda, M.
  - Journal: MNRAS, 2023
  - Role: **MID** (2nd author)
  - Summary: Mean-field dynamo + wind-braking model shows that a change in latitudinal rotation profile near the solar age reduces the magnetic-braking torque, matching the observed plateau in gyrochronology.

- **arXiv:2211.04883** — "Role of Longitudinal Waves in Alfvén-wave-driven Solar/Stellar Wind" (conference proceedings)
  - Authors: Shimizu, K., Shoda, M., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Venue: IAU 370 Proceedings, 2022
  - Role: **LAST**
  - Summary: Proceedings companion to 2203.15280 — summarizes how compressive (longitudinal) waves channel a non-negligible fraction of the acceleration budget in Alfvén-wave-driven winds.

- **arXiv:2211.01072** — "Three-dimensional Simulations of Magnetospheric Accretion in a T Tauri Star: Accretion and Wind Structures Just Around Star"
  - Authors: Takasao, S., Tomida, K., Iwasaki, K., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: ApJ, 2023
  - Role: **LAST**
  - Summary: Global 3D MHD of a dipolar stellar magnetosphere + accretion disk resolves funnel accretion and conical disk winds simultaneously, reproducing the observed spectroscopic signatures of classical T Tauri stars.

- **arXiv:2209.10156** — "The Effect of the Chromospheric Temperature on Coronal Heating"
  - Authors: Washinoue, H., Shoda, M., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: ApJ, 2022
  - Role: **LAST**
  - Summary: 1D chromosphere-to-corona wave simulations show that lower chromospheric temperatures (as in Pop. II stars) change the wave transmission/reflection balance and lead to systematically cooler coronae.

- **arXiv:2203.15280** — "Role of Longitudinal Waves in Alfvén-wave-driven Solar Wind"
  - Authors: Shimizu, K., Shoda, M., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: ApJ, 2022
  - Role: **LAST**
  - Summary: Demonstrates via 1D direct-compressible-MHD wind models that parametric-decay-generated sound waves contribute ~30 % of the heating budget beyond ~10 R☉, comparable to nonlinear Alfvén-wave cascade.

- **arXiv:2201.08840** — "Rapid-then-slow migration reproduces mass distribution of TRAPPIST-1 system"
  - Authors: Ogihara, M., Kokubo, E., Nakano, R., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: A&A, 2022
  - Role: **LAST** (senior co-author)
  - Summary: N-body planet-formation simulations with a time-dependent migration rate (fast in gas-rich phase, slow after disk dispersal) reproduce the observed near-equal TRAPPIST-1 planet masses.

## 2021

  - Authors: Washinoue, H., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: MNRAS (accepted), 2021
  - Role: **LAST**
  - Summary: 1D Alfvén-wave-driven wind + coronal model for zero-metal low-mass stars predicts X-ray/UV fluxes that could contribute a non-negligible ionization feedback in the early universe.

- **arXiv:2104.06711** — "Collisional Growth and Fragmentation of Dust Aggregates with Low Mass Ratios. I: Critical Collision Velocity for Water Ice"
  - Authors: Hasegawa, Y., Suzuki, T. K., Tanaka, H., Kobayashi, H., Wada, K.
  - Journal: ApJ (accepted), 2021
  - Role: **MID** (2nd author)
  - Summary: Monomer-resolving N-body collision experiments of icy dust aggregates calibrate the sticking-vs-fragmentation threshold velocity as a function of aggregate mass ratio for use in protoplanetary-disk models.

- **arXiv:2103.07673** — "Photoevaporative Dispersal of Protoplanetary Disks around Evolving Intermediate-mass Stars"
  - Authors: Kunitomo, M., Ida, S., Takeuchi, T., Panić, O., Miley, J. M., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: ApJ, 2021
  - Role: **LAST**
  - Summary: Combines stellar-evolution-driven XEUV luminosity with 1D disk evolution; shows that intermediate-mass (Herbig) stars clear their disks via photoevaporation faster than low-mass T Tauri analogs.

## 2020

- **arXiv:2005.09817** — "Alfvén-wave driven magnetic rotator winds from low-mass stars I: rotation dependences of magnetic braking and mass-loss rate"
  - Authors: Shoda, M., Suzuki, T. K., Matt, S. P., Cranmer, S. R., Vidotto, A. A., Strugarek, A., See, V., Réville, V., Finley, A. J., Brun, A. S.
  - Journal: ApJ, 2020
  - Role: **MID** (2nd author)
  - Summary: A wide parameter scan of 2.5D Alfvén-wave-driven-wind simulations over rotation rate yields a torque scaling that transitions from unsaturated (Ω^3) to saturated, broadly consistent with gyrochronology data.

- **arXiv:2004.08839** — "New growth mechanism of dust grains in protoplanetary disks with magnetically driven disk winds"
  - Authors: Taki, T., Kuwabara, K., Kobayashi, H., Suzuki, T. K.
  - Journal: ApJ (accepted), 2020
  - Role: **LAST**
  - Summary: Proposes that wind-driven radial mass flux concentrates dust near local pressure bumps, giving a new pathway to planetesimal formation that bypasses the classic meter-size barrier.

- **arXiv:2001.03949** — "Dispersal of protoplanetary disks by the combination of magnetically driven and photoevaporative winds"
  - Authors: Kunitomo, M., Suzuki, T. K., Inutsuka, S.
  - Journal: MNRAS, 2020
  - Role: **MID** (2nd author)
  - Summary: 1D viscous-disk models including both MHD and photoevaporative mass-loss channels reproduce the observed 3 Myr disk-dispersal timescale and predict a two-phase (inside-out, then rapid) clearing.
